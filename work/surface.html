brand purpose

  • Purpose: We believe that fashion is a powerful medium of self-expression where we design to feel as good as it looks, inspired by the artists they admire. Where they feel connected to the world of fame.
  • Process: We achieve this by crafting innovative, surface-designed fashion that is bold, edgy, and inspired by music, art, and pop culture. collaborating with artists and tastemakers to create exclusive, trend-setting pieces.
  • Result: The result is unique, artist-driven clothing that allows people to connect with fame and express their identity through bold, standout pieces.
